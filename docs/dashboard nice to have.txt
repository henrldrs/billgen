1. Essential Dashboard Cards ("Should Have")
These cards provide the core daily operational status and basic financial visibility.

A. Agenda & Billing Pipeline
Unbilled Appointments & Ready-to-Bill Card:

Shows total appointments completed in the current billing cycle that haven't been invoiced yet.

Displays a monetary estimate based on appointment rates + added extra items.

Action: Quick "Review & Generate Drafts" button.

Upcoming Appointments (Agenda Overview):

A compact view of today's and this week's scheduled client appointments.

Shows client name, service type, and whether it's confirmed or pending.

B. Core Financial Metrics (Top KPI Row)
Monthly Revenue (Current vs. Previous Month):

Total billed revenue this month with percentage growth relative to the previous month.

Outstanding & Overdue Invoices:

Total unpaid balance split into Pending (Within Terms) and Overdue (Late).

Badge/counter for invoices requiring reminders.

Estimated Tax & VAT Buffer:

Real-time calculation of VAT collected and estimated income tax to set aside for the quarter/year.

C. Offerings & Catalog Breakdown
Revenue Split: Services vs. Products (Visual Chart):

A stacked bar or donut chart comparing revenue from Services (hourly fees, consultation appointments, recurring retainers) versus Products (digital goods, software licenses, physical materials, flat-rate items).

D. Quick Actions Widget
One-click shortcuts: + New Appointment, + Quick Invoice, + Add Product/Service, + Manual Line Item.

A. Automated Monthly Billing Queue (The Agenda Hub)
What it does: Acts as an intelligent staging area for your end-of-month batch billing.

Key Details:

Displays clients whose monthly appointments have closed.

Shows itemized previews: X Appointments + Y Extra Products/Items added during sessions.

Batch Actions: "Approve All & Send Invoices", "Edit Line Items", or "Snooze Client to Next Month".

B. Cash Flow & Revenue Forecast (Billed + Scheduled)
What it does: Combines past invoice data with future scheduled agenda appointments.

Key Details:

Shows guaranteed income (issued invoices) + projected income (confirmed upcoming appointments in the agenda for the next 30/60 days).

Helps the freelancer see if they will hit their monthly income target before the month ends.

C. Effective Hourly Rate & Admin Overhead Tracker
What it does: Calculates the true profitability of services.

Key Details:

Divides total service revenue by hours spent (billable session time vs. non-billable setup/admin time).

Indicates if certain service types or clients are yielding a lower effective hourly rate than your base rate.

D. Product vs. Service Profitability & Velocity Matrix
What it does: Provides deep insight into your product catalog versus active service time.

Key Details:

Top Service: Most booked service type by volume and revenue.

Top Product/Add-on: Most frequently added item attached to appointments (e.g., hosting fees, specialized software licenses, physical supplies).

Passive vs. Active Ratio: Percentage of income earned from products (zero extra labor) vs. services (labored time).

E. Client Revenue Concentration & Health Index
What it does: Protects the freelancer from financial risk by tracking client dependency.

Key Details:

Displays client income distribution (e.g., "Client X represents 55% of your total revenue this quarter").

Flags warning status if a single client dominates cash flow, helping plan diversification.

F. "Safe-to-Spend" Real-Time Cash Indicator
What it does: Takes total bank/invoice receipts and automatically deducts estimated quarterly VAT, income tax reserve, and recurring operating expenses.

Key Details:

Gives the freelancer one bold number: "Safe Personal Draw: $X,XXX", preventing accidental over-spending before tax deadlines.

Key UI Enhancements
1. Upgraded Top KPI Bar

Keep: Invoiced (€0.00), Paid (€0.00), Outstanding (€0.00), Overdue (0).

Add: Unbilled Agenda (€0.00) — Calculates total value of completed appointments in the current month awaiting billing.

Add: Estimated Tax Buffer — Displays VAT/Income tax set-aside so you know your net income.

2. Actionable Empty States
Replace "No invoices were issued in this year" with contextual quick actions:

Current: No invoices were issued in this year.

Better: No invoices issued for 2026 yet. [Schedule an Appointment] or [Create Manual Invoice]

3. Dual-Category Revenue Chart (Services vs. Products)
Upgrade the Revenue by month — 2026 component from a basic list into a visual chart that separates your offerings:

Stacked Bar View: Shows month-by-month revenue split into Services (Session time, hourly) and Products (One-off items, materials, licenses).

Dotted/Ghost Bars: Displays projected revenue from confirmed appointments scheduled in your agenda for the rest of the month.

4. Agenda & Unbilled Staging Widget
Place a dedicated section directly above your main charts:

Displays appointments confirmed as "Done" that need monthly invoice generation.

Shows extra line items attached during the appointment (e.g., Service: 1h Coaching + Item: Physical Handbook).

Batch Action Button: "Generate & Send Monthly Invoices".

5. Activity Log Relocation

Move the raw timestamps (Created 29/08/2026...) to a smaller lower-right card or a collapsible side drawer.

Enriched log formatting: Created Invoice #102 for Client X or Added Product 'Hosting' to Appointment #4 instead of generic Created/Edited text.
